Privacy notice

This notice explains the main information used by Luo Chat and your choices when using the service.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Information used to provide the service

  • Account details: Email address, username, profile information and account identifiers when you register or update your profile. Authentication and password management use Supabase Auth; you should create a separate password for Luo Chat.
  • Conversations: Messages, conversation membership, attachments and the associated delivery or conversation metadata needed for messaging features.
  • Optional features: Media, device notification subscriptions, device permissions and preferences when you choose to use those features.
  • Browser storage: Session data, drafts, preferences, and, if enabled, recent chat text or files saved locally for offline use. Some local copies can remain until you clear them or sign out, depending on settings.

Where information is handled

Luo Chat uses Supabase for authentication, application data and permitted media storage, and Vercel to host the web app. Browsers and push providers may also process the technical information needed for notifications or app installation.

Who can see content?

In-app permissions restrict access based on accounts, conversation membership and feature settings. Private content is not intentionally placed in the public PWA shell cache. This is not a promise that the service is end-to-end encrypted; do not treat Luo Chat as an end-to-end encrypted messenger.

Choices and deletion

You can change available account, privacy, notification and device-storage settings in the app. The app includes an account-deletion flow and controls for clearing local copies. Deleting an account or message may involve asynchronous cleanup; recipients or independent downloaded copies may remain. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or retention, contact support.

Security and permissions

Camera, microphone, files and notifications are optional device features. Browsers ask for permission where required. Never bypass a Chrome security warning to sign in or install the app. Do not share your Luo Chat password, recovery links or secret tokens with support.

Questions

For privacy enquiries contact creationslous@gmail.com. This page will be updated if material data-handling practices change.
