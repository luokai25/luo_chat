Using Luo Chat

For account, messaging and installation help, visit Help center. To test public PWA files, see Connection checks.

How do people find me?

Share your exact username or your profile link. People can find you from the People tab.

How do I manage a group?

Open the conversation menu, then Members. Owners can promote admins and transfer ownership.

Where are my messages stored?

Messages and private attachments are stored in Supabase with access rules. Local drafts stay in your browser. End-to-end encryption is not included.

Why is my icon still old?

Close and reopen the app. Your operating system decides when installed icons refresh; if it stays old, remove the home-screen shortcut and install the app again.
